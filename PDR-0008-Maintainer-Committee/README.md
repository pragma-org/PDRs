## Context
The [Statutes](https://github.com/pragma-org/PDRs/blob/main/.incorporation/20240422_Attachment_Statutes_Statuten_PRAGMA_signed.pdf) clearly call out the need for Maintainer Committees and Leads therein. However that information is spread throughout the statutes and at times leaves room for ambiguity. They organize, manage and implement a software project and at times need guidance on the expectations and requirements within PRAGMA. 

## Motivation
Clearly defining the structure and responsibilities of the Maintainer Committees and their Leads are essential for accountability, transparency and efficiency.

## Decision
Maintainer Committees
- Composition: The Maintainer Committee is self organized and comprised of individuals who have demonstrated consistent contributions to a PRAGMA project. The Lead appoints the other members. Thereafter members are appointed by co-optation, new members are selected based on merit and are voted in by existing members of the committee. Maintainer committees will elect new Leads as needed based on merit and vote by existing members of the committee.  
- Removal of members: A Maintainer Committee may remove a member without a decision, approval or ratification by the Administrative Board, using the following process:
        - The member will receive written notice of the proposed removal and its reasons, and at least 7 calendar days to respond before the committee decides.
    - Removal may only be based on prolonged inactivity, repeated breach of the committee's approved regulations, or a finding under PDR-0010. Conduct allegations are handled through PDR-0010, not through this process.
    - The member being considered for removal may not vote on the decision. Any other member with a conflict of interest must declare it before the vote, using the categories defined in PDR-0010:
      - A hard conflict (being the person proposing or affected by the removal in a personal capacity, having a close personal or professional relationship with the member being considered for removal, or having another personal interest likely to undermine impartiality) requires the member to recuse themselves.
      - A soft conflict (working at the same company as the member being considered for removal without a close relationship, or other circumstances that give the appearance of a conflict) must be declared and recorded; the Administrative Board decides whether recusal is required.
    - Removing the Lead requires a unanimous vote in favor from all other eligible committee members. Abstention or silence does not count as agreement.
    - Removing another member requires yes votes from at least two thirds of the members eligible to vote, not only of those who vote.
    - The committee will document the decision, including the vote, reasons and effective date, notify the member and the Administrative Board and update its membership record. Notice to the Board is for information and is not a condition of the decision taking effect.
This process does not limit the Board’s separate authority under Article 22(1)(i) of the Statutes or the dispute resolution provisions of Article 32. If fewer than two Maintainer Committee members are eligible to vote on a proposed removal, the matter will be referred to the Board.
- Structure: They report to the Administrative Board in phase 1. In phase 2 (start to be decided by the General Assembly) Project Committees will be implemented and assume that reporting role.
- Responsibilities:  
  - Code Review and Quality Assurance: Ensuring that all code contributions meet the project's standards and adhere to any PRAGMA policies.
  - Issue Triage: Prioritizing and addressing issues reported by the community.
  - Release Management: Coordinating and managing project releases.
  - Documentation: Maintaining and updating project documentation.
  - Community Engagement: Interacting with users and contributors to foster a healthy community.
  - Funding: Procure their own funding and be transparent with third parties about intended use of the funds, the Maintainer Committee structure and open source nature of the project and the rules and limitations imposed by PRAGMA. 

Maintainer Committee Leads
 - Selection: The initial Lead of a Maintainer Committee is appointed by the Administrative Board. Subsequent Leads are appointed by co-optation in accordance with Article 27(1) of the Statutes, a vote by existing members of the committee.
 - Responsibilities:
   - Initial Members: Upon appointment, the Lead appoints the other initial members. 
   - Initial Setup: Document the Committees organization, responsibilities and authority and submit it to the Administrative Board for approval expeditiously upon appointment. 
   - Facilitation: Organizing and leading MC meetings and discussions.
   - Conflict Resolution: Addressing and mediating disputes within the MC or among contributors.
   - Delegation: Assist as needed in assigning tasks and ensuring fair distribution of responsibilities among MC members while keeping the project moving forward. 
   - Representation: Serving as the primary liaison between the MC and the Administrative Board or other committees. 
     - They will work with the Administrative Board regarding services, such as Corporate housekeeping support, Intellectual Property support, etc. 
     - They will aid the MC in keeping in compliance with PRAGMA rules and regulations, to include licensing, security, etc. 
   - Reports: Submitting quarterly reports to the Administrative Board, due 5 days before the end of a quarter.


## Consequences

### Positive outcomes
Structured Leadership: Clear delineation of roles fosters an effective workflow.
Collaboration: Defined responsibilities promote better teamwork.
Improved Decision-Making: A formalized structure aids in prompt and transparent decisions.
